## Scope
Share of voice analysis, domain comparison, rank tracking, competitive positioning.

## Key Concepts (to be expanded)
- Share of Voice: percentage of top-10 clicks for target keywords that go to your site vs competitors
- Domain comparison: traffic, keyword count, DR/DA, backlink profile side by side
- Rank tracking: monitor position changes for target keywords over time
- Competitor content analysis: what topics they cover, content formats used, update frequency
- Competitor technical analysis: site speed, schema usage, mobile experience
- Competitive gap identification: where competitors are strong and you're weak (and vice versa)
