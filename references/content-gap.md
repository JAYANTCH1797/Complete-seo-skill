## Scope
Competitor URL diffing, missing topic detection, keyword gap identification.

## Key Concepts (to be expanded)
- Find keywords competitors rank for (positions 1-50) that you don't rank for at all
- Filter by: high traffic potential, low-to-medium difficulty, high business relevance
- Map gaps to buyer journey stages (awareness, consideration, decision)
- Identify content format gaps (competitors have videos/tools/guides you lack)
- Subtopic gaps within existing content (competitors cover angles you missed)

## Tools
- Semrush MCP: organic_research (competitor keywords), keyword_research (gap filtering)
- Manual: Compare top-ranking pages' subtopics against your content
