## Scope
Generate actionable content briefs from keyword + SERP analysis + content gap data.

## Key Concepts (to be expanded)
- Target keyword + search intent (the 3 Cs: type, format, angle)
- SERP analysis: what top 10 pages cover, their structure, word count range
- Subtopics to cover: from competitor analysis, PAA questions, related keywords
- Content format recommendation: based on what ranks
- Unique angle: what can this content offer that competitors don't?
- E-E-A-T requirements: author credentials needed, experience signals, sources to cite
- Internal linking targets: existing pages to link from/to
- Featured snippet opportunity: specific section formatting
- Word count guidance: based on competitive analysis (not arbitrary minimums)

## Tools
- Semrush MCP: keyword_research, organic_research
- WebFetch: analyze top-ranking competitor pages
- scripts/serp_scraper.py: SERP feature extraction
- assets/templates/content_brief_template.md: output template
